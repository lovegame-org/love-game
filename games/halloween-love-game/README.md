# Halloween Love Game

A seasonal free browser love / couple pick with a Halloween twist. Same light vibe as Love Tester-style games, dressed up for spooky season — play instantly in the browser.

## How to play

1. Open the game and follow the on-screen prompts.
2. Enter names or make a choice when asked.
3. Enjoy the Halloween-themed result or mini-interaction.

**Tip:** Best as a quick share with a partner or friend during October events.
