# Love Dots

A free browser puzzle where you connect hearts / dots to clear the board. Calm, couple-friendly pacing — good for short sessions on phone or desktop.

## How to play

1. Look at the dots / hearts on the board.
2. Connect matching pairs with a continuous path (rules shown in-game).
3. Clear levels without trapping yourself.

**Tip:** Plan one move ahead — blocking a path early is the usual fail.

[Play Love Dots on love-game.org](https://love-game.org/games/love-dots/)
