# Fireboy and Watergirl: Light Temple

A free 2-player co-op browser temple run. Control Fireboy and Watergirl together — light puzzles, switches, and elemental hazards. Ideal for couples or friends on one keyboard.

## How to play

1. Fireboy usually uses arrow keys; Watergirl uses WASD (confirm on the game screen).
2. Reach both exits: fire stays off water, water stays off lava.
3. Use levers, boxes, and light puzzles to open paths.

**Tip:** Talk out loud — most fails are one player rushing while the other is stuck.
